## 2. Stability-evidence benches (`src/ae/stability.py`; run on the adopted baseline only, no campaign rerun; `runs/ref/stability/`, `docs/figures/stability_evidence.png`)
**Loop gain** - single series voltage injection **at each op-amp's inverting-input pin**, T = -V(n)/V(nA). Why the input pin: voltage injection is exact only if the receiving impedance >> the driving one. A first version broke the loop at the op-amp *output*; there the stage-1 feedback network is C2 = 53 ohm at the 3 MHz crossover, *below* the model's output impedance, so the assumption fails where the margin is read. That version read **PM 70.3 deg / GM 14.8 dB (stage 1)**, optimistic by ~3.5 deg / ~2.7 dB versus the valid input-pin result. (The first run also showed PM 250 deg: bug 4 above.)
| | crossover | phase margin | gain margin (phase crossover) | DC loop gain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 MFB (U1A), input-pin injection | 3.03 MHz | **66.7 deg** | **12.0 dB** (8.37 MHz) | 103.9 dB (10 Hz) |
| Stage 2 1st-order (U1B), input-pin injection | 1.80 MHz | **61.4 deg** | **17.5 dB** (8.14 MHz) | 105.3 dB (10 Hz) |
**Input step** (0.2 V at the source behind Rs = 100 ohm) vs the SAME netlist with ideal op-amps (VCVS 1e6): output step ~253 mV (AC-coupled droop included); overshoot real 11.46 % vs ideal 11.34 % (the filter's own); **max |real - ideal| 0.54 mV = 0.21 % of the step**; residual p-p of the difference in the second half of the window 0.9 uV.
**Load step** (50 uA sink pulse on AOUT, input quiet) vs ideal op-amps: static droop -23.76 mV (real) vs -23.75 (ideal, = I x R6); **overshoot beyond static 0.37 mV (1.6 % of the step), 1 sign change outside a 0.05 mV band**, recovery to band 0.76 us (ideal 0.95 us, no overshoot); release returns to within 0.003 mV.
**How to read it - and its limits.** At nominal parts/temperature the measured loops have 61-67 deg / 12-17 dB margins and the real op-amp adds <= 0.5 mV (input step) and a 0.37 mV ripple (load step) relative to ideal ones. That is *evidence*, not a verdict: no tolerance corners (R6, C5, filter parts), no temperature, no extra load capacitance beyond R6/C5 (the J2 cable/ADC board is unknown), a single amplitude per step, and the shimmed model whose open-loop PM is 58 deg vs the datasheet's 65 deg and whose **startup, saturation and overload behaviour are unvalidated** (`MODEL_*` checks stay `not_tested`). Nothing here removes the REQ-STAB failure or supports rev 3 as a stability test.

## 3. Capacitor roles: the actual requirement is effective capacitance under bias, not a voltage ratio
My earlier "derating warning" for the 10 V parts on 5.25 V used a 50 % rule of thumb that is **not a contract requirement and not from Kemet**. It is now a neutral *rating margin note*; a part is not failed for it. The real requirement per role is: C_eff(DC bias, temperature, age) >= C_min(role), where C_min comes from what the circuit needs. Measured with the TI model (`runs/ref/review/capbias/`): 
| Role | What it does | Tolerated loss (measured) | Derived C_min | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1, C2, C3, C5 (C0G, 50 V) | filter/bucket | class I: no DC-bias loss mechanism claimed by the sheet | tolerance +-5 % is already in the corners | Kemet part sheets (rating, tolerance, dielectric); no bias plot needed for C0G |
| C4 2.2 uF X7R 16 V, ~1.65 V bias | AC coupling (high-pass ~1.1 Hz) | flatness unaffected down to ~15 % of nominal; 0.5 dB limit reached at ~7-8 % (nominal) / ~8 % (worst LP corner); Zin never binding (58 kohm) | C4_eff >= ~0.18 uF (~8 %) | **bias curve MISSING** (Kemet PDF has notes only; K-SIM online); headroom is >90 % loss, so low risk, but unquantified |
| C6 10 uF X7R 10 V, ~1.65 V bias | VMID bias decoupling | integrated noise unchanged even with C6 removed (0.0097 -> 0.0103 mV; limit 0.2); but **VREF->AOUT coupling worsens 6 dB per halving**: -35.9 dB @50 Hz at 10 uF, -17.3 dB at 10 %, positive gain at 1 kHz with ~0 | **none: the contract has no VREF-ripple/noise requirement** | requirement MISSING + bias curve MISSING |
| C7 10 uF X7R 10 V (5.25 V applied = 52 % of rating), C8 100 nF X7R 16 V | supply decoupling | **not testable**: the supply is an ideal source in every bench | none: no supply impedance/ripple/PSRR requirement | requirement MISSING + bias curve MISSING; both stay `unresolved`-adjacent, noted by BOM_CONSISTENCY |
To close the gaps: obtain Kemet's capacitance-vs-DC-bias data for C1206C225K4RACTU and C1206C106K8RACTU (and C0603C104K4RACTU), then either (a) accept C_min values above, or (b) have the owner add a VREF-ripple requirement and a supply-impedance assumption so C6/C7/C8 get a pass/fail threshold. Neither is done; nothing is approved.

## 4. Gain margin: "inherent" is retracted
Decomposition of the adopted design's 400 Hz gain spread over the contract tolerance box (ideal model, `runs/ref/review/gainmargin/decompose.py`): resistor ratios -3.87 % / +4.02 % (analytic bound -3.92 % / +4.08 %), + R3 -3.91 % / +4.07 %, filter capacitors alone +-0.5 %, Rs and C4 +0.17 %, full box -4.44 % / +4.80 % (spread 0.1202 of the 0.130 window). The ratios alone would leave ~0.9 % (~0.012 V/V) per side. Search (`search.py`, 100 E96 selections with nominal fit < 0.25 dB, nominal gain 1.292-1.306): the best gain margin is **+0.0053** (R1 59.0k, R2 76.8k, R3 5.76k, R4=R5 16.2k; nominal 1.2978), about 3x the adopted +0.0018 (ideal-model), but its worst flatness is 0.478 dB (margin +0.022 vs +0.087 adopted); the next candidates trade 0.007-0.021 dB of flatness margin for ~+0.005 gain margin, one violates flatness (0.535). So the thin margin is a property of this selection and of a flatness/gain trade within the +-1 % / +-5 % class, not a demonstrated limit of +-1 % resistors in general. These are ideal-model estimates; none was simulated with the real model or adopted.
